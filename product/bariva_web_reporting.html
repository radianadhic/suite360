Bagi UU PDP No. 27/2022, data keuangan penduduk Indonesia wajib berada di wilayah Indonesia. Itu bukan pilihan gaya bagi Bariva — satu berkas terpasang, tumpukan terbuka, tanpa lisensi per modul.

Under Law No. 27/2022 on personal data protection, the financial data of Indonesian residents must stay in Indonesia. For Bariva that is not a stylistic choice: one deployable artefact, an open stack, no per-module licensing.
